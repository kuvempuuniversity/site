Business Administration

Established in 1991 initially at the Davangere PG Centre before moving to our sprawling green campus at Jnana Sahyadri, Shankaraghatta, in 2005, the Institute of Management Studies and Research (IMSR) offers comprehensive full-time M.B.A. and specialized M.B.A. in Human Resources Management (M.B.A.-HRM) programs, alongside doctoral research opportunities. Recognized nationally in prominent B-School surveys, our institute combines modern smart classrooms, virtual and physical libraries, and outbound experiential learning programs to develop ethical business leaders. Supported by qualified faculty, industry expert lectures, active seminar series, and a strong alumni network, we equip our students with essential managerial and soft skills to secure rewarding placements across diverse corporate sectors.
