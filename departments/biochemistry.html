Biochemistry

Established during the 2005-06 academic year at the Shankaraghatta main campus, our department offers M.Sc., M.Phil., and Ph.D. programs in Biochemistry designed to provide rigorous theoretical training and hands-on practical methods. Guided by experienced faculty and inspired by special lecture series from accomplished scientists, our students develop the competencies needed for careers in academic research, pharmaceutical R&D, diagnostic labs, and biotech industries. Driven by our vision to be a centre of excellence in basic and applied biochemistry, we are committed to delivering value-based education and fostering independent, highly competent graduates ready to tackle global technological challenges.
