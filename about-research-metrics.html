About this website and research metrics

A student-first website concept. Research figures describe available records, not a university ranking or a complete institutional research assessment.

Whose research is represented?

Department collections combine the teaching team and published PhD-student profiles listed on this website. Publications can predate a researcher’s appointment or originate at other institutions. These figures must not be interpreted as research conducted exclusively at Kuvempu University.

Publication and PhD figures

The research paper counter includes source-classified journal articles, reviews and full conference papers. Other outputs remain listed but are excluded from this counter. Matching shared papers are counted once within a department. Metadata and coverage can be incomplete. PhDs awarded are reported separately from verified departmental records, not inferred from researchers’ qualifications or current student profiles. An unavailable total is shown as a dash, not zero.

Environmental Science: Google Scholar citations

For Environmental Science, citation counts are retrieved from recorded public Google Scholar profiles through the third-party provider SerpApi, not an official Google API. Retrieved articles are matched to the existing departmental research-paper collection using exact normalised titles and publication years, supported by author-name overlap. This is automated matching, not independent verification of every record.

The initial snapshot, retrieved on 31 October 2026 at 20:11 IST (14:41 UTC), contains 1,788 citations across 45 matched papers out of 72 eligible papers. Five public profiles were retrieved using seven provider requests. Thirteen listed researchers lacked a usable recorded Scholar profile; successful retrieval of the five available profiles does not establish complete departmental coverage.

Each matched paper contributes once, including when it occurs on multiple researchers’ profiles. Individual author-profile totals are not added together. The total sums citations to papers; it is not a count of distinct citing documents across the whole department. Unmatched records, unavailable values and conflicting observations are excluded, not treated as confirmed zero-citation papers. Google Scholar values are neither added to nor silently replaced by OpenAlex counts.

The public label is “Citations (Google Scholar)”. Paper-level counts retain source links, retrieval dates and an automatically matched label. If the bibliography changes, only snapshot records that still match the current eligible paper collection contribute. This paper-level snapshot is not automatically refreshed.

Other departments and comparison limits

Other departments continue to use available validated, cached OpenAlex citation counts for their eligible research papers. Source coverage, matching and retrieval dates differ, so citation totals from different sources are not directly comparable. A higher total is not by itself evidence of greater research impact.
