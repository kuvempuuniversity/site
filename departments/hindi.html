Hindi

Established in December 1993, our department offers postgraduate and research programs in Hindi, fostering an engaging academic environment through innovative syllabus design, participatory audio-visual teaching methods, and special lecture series. Supported by a departmental library housing over 500 books, two instituted gold medals for academic excellence, and dedicated faculty-led coaching classes for competitive exams, our students consistently clear NET and SLET examinations to build successful careers in teaching and research.
