Economics

Established in 1974-75 under the University of Mysore before becoming one of Kuvempu University's oldest postgraduate departments in 1987, our department has a four-decade legacy of excellence in economic education and research. Catering to students from diverse socio-cultural backgrounds through adaptable teaching methods, guest lecture series by visiting experts, and student-faculty forums like the Planning Forum, we combine rigorous theoretical instruction with practical training and completed major UGC-funded research projects.
