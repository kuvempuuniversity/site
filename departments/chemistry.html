Chemistry

Established in 1992, our department is actively involved in advanced academic and research programs in electrochemistry, coordination chemistry, synthetic organic chemistry, corrosion and metal finishing, nanotechnology, and synthetic dyes and pigments in partnership with industries and research institutions. We offer a Choice Based Credit System for M.Sc. students and have successfully completed DST-FIST programs alongside receiving significant grants under UGC-SAP and non-SAP schemes. Backed by funding from agencies like the UGC, DST, and SERB, our faculty have published over a thousand research papers in reputed journals and guided more than 150 Ph.D. scholars, while our students consistently clear competitive exams like NET, K-SET, and GATE and secure rewarding career placements through dedicated campus interviews with leading pharmaceutical industries and R&D organizations.
