No fresh citation retrieval, paid provider calls or automatic selection jobs are enabled by this page.

Rules before publishing verified university-wide selections
  1. Reconcile researcher identities, department assignments and eligible roles against the university roster.
  2. Use the same citation source and reporting scope within each comparison.
  3. Agree reporting periods, eligible output types, minimum coverage and treatment of ties.
  4. Display sources, retrieval dates and denominators. Missing records must not silently become zeroes.
  5. Review selections before calling them university-wide leaders. These metrics are not field-normalised measures of research quality.
