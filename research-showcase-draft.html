How to read this working draft

These are replaceable examples, not verified institutional performance selections. Profile totals, paper-level citation counts, citations per paper, and citations per staff member are distinct measures and are not silently substituted for one another. No fresh citation retrieval, paid provider calls or automatic selection jobs are enabled by this page.

Rules before publishing verified university-wide selections
  1. Reconcile researcher identities, department assignments and eligible roles against the university roster.
  2. Use the same citation source and reporting scope within each comparison. Do not divide all-time Scholar profile citations by an unrelated ORCID publication count.
  3. Agree reporting periods, eligible output types, minimum paper counts, minimum data coverage and treatment of ties.
  4. For departmental paper totals, deduplicate shared papers. For citations per staff member, verify both the eligible staff-linked publication collection and staff denominator.
  5. Display sources, retrieval dates, collection scope and denominators. Missing records must not silently become zeroes.
  6. Review selections before calling them university-wide leaders. These metrics are not field-normalised measures or complete assessments of research quality.
Attribution and interpretation

Researcher records may include work completed before joining Kuvempu University or while associated with another institution. The examples describe the scholarly records of the listed researchers, not exclusively work produced at the University.

The eventual showcase can recognise several kinds of scholarly contribution rather than declaring an overall best researcher or department. Books, datasets, research stories and policy or community contributions can be considered as supporting evidence becomes available.

Existing department pages, source citation datasets, retrieval schedules and main navigation are unchanged by this draft-page update.
