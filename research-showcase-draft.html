University-wide research · Working draft

Research showcase

Recognising research influence, scholarly service and departmental contributions across Kuvempu University.

Working draft created on 1 November 2026. This is a page for collaborative development, not a published performance ranking. No top-three selections have been calculated or approved. Names and figures will be added only after profile updates, data coverage and the comparison rules have been reviewed.

Proposed highlight · Three selections

Highly cited papers and their researchers

Highlight three distinct papers with the highest available citation counts, alongside the names and departments of their associated university researchers.

Draft basis: citation count of an individual paper. A shared paper appears once, with its relevant co-authors credited. A researcher’s highest-cited paper is the maximum citation count within that person’s eligible collection; this is different from their average citations per paper.

Paper and researcher selections pending reviewed data. No names or citation totals published yet.

Proposed highlight · Three researchers

Citations per research paper

Recognise researchers whose eligible paper collections have high average citation counts, with paper numbers and data coverage shown alongside the result.

Draft calculation: citations to eligible papers with available counts ÷ number of those same papers. Confirmed zero-citation papers are included; unknown counts are not treated as zero. Minimum paper-count and coverage requirements remain to be agreed.

Researcher selections pending consistent citation coverage and agreed eligibility rules.

Proposed highlight · Three researchers

Recorded peer-review contributions

Celebrate contributions to scholarly evaluation using publicly recorded peer-review activities retrieved from ORCID.

Draft basis: distinct public peer-review activities, not review articles or the number of journal groups. Recorded activity is not a complete lifetime reviewing total; visibility and recognition practices affect coverage. Peer-review retrieval and deduplication are planned, not yet implemented for this page.

Selections pending ORCID peer-review retrieval, source checks and review of duplicate credits.

Proposed highlight · Three departments

Departmental citations per staff member

Explore departmental research influence relative to staff numbers, using a clearly defined and consistently applied staff cohort.

Draft calculation: citations to unique eligible staff-linked papers ÷ eligible staff headcount. Do not divide the website’s combined staff-and-student citation total by staff numbers alone. An alternative is citations per listed researcher, with both staff and students included in the denominator.

Department selections pending verified headcounts, comparable citation data and an agreed definition of eligible staff.

Rules to agree before publishing selections

  1. Complete and review researcher identities, department assignments and profile records. A person listed in more than one role must not be counted twice in headcounts.
  2. Use a consistent paper-level citation source for a comparison. The current website uses Google Scholar for Environmental Science and OpenAlex for other departments; those totals must not be combined into an apparently uniform university-wide ranking.
  3. Choose the reporting period and distinguish papers published within a period from citations received within that period. Keep all-time and recent highlights separate.
  4. Define eligible output types, staff categories, minimum paper counts, minimum data coverage and treatment of ties. Decisions on guest faculty and research scholars who teach are still pending.
  5. Deduplicate shared papers within department and university totals. Identify co-authors rather than presenting one shared paper as several independent achievements.
  6. Display source, retrieval date, citation coverage, eligible paper count and the denominator used. Incomplete records must not silently become zeroes.
  7. Review proposed selections before publication. Raw citations, citations per paper and citations per staff member are not field-normalised measures and should not be presented as a complete assessment of research quality.
Attribution and interpretation

Researcher records can include work completed before joining Kuvempu University or while associated with another institution. Until affiliation and publication dates are verified, the page should describe the scholarly records of the listed researchers, not claim that every output was produced at the University.

This showcase should recognise several kinds of scholarly contribution rather than declare an overall best researcher or department. Editorial research stories, books, datasets and policy or community contributions can be considered in later versions when suitable evidence is available.

No retrieval jobs, paid provider calls, automatic rankings or scheduled updates are enabled by this draft. Existing department pages, counts and citation datasets remain unchanged.
