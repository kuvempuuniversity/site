Social Work

Our department offers a professionally oriented Master of Social Work (M.S.W.) program designed to cater to the needs of individuals, groups, and communities across three core specializations: Community Development, Medical and Psychiatric Social Work, and Human Resource Management. Tailored for students committed to human service and social empowerment, our curriculum combines rigorous coursework with hands-on experiential learning, including field work practicums, orientation visits to developmental organizations, hospitals, and industries, a one-week rural camp, a one-month block placement internship, and regular workshops and seminars.
