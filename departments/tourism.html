Tourism

Established in the academic year 2005-06, our department offers a multidisciplinary professional MBA program in Tourism and Travel Management [MBA (TTM)] alongside doctoral research opportunities. Designed to meet global industry standards, our comprehensive curriculum covers national and international travel and tourism, hotel and hospitality management, airline management, event management, and essential business and computer skills. To bridge classroom learning with practical industry requirements, we regularly invite tourism and allied sector experts for interactive guest lectures, organize regional and national study tours, and mandate a one-month on-the-job training program alongside a fourth-semester dissertation, ensuring our students graduate fully prepared for dynamic careers in the global tourism and hospitality industry.
