Biotechnology

Established in 1992 as the Department of Life Science before being re-designated as the Department of Biotechnology in 2000, our department focuses on bio-prospecting medicinal plants, genomics, proteomics, plant genetic engineering, and agricultural entomology. We offer postgraduate and doctoral programs—alongside pioneering state initiatives like the first M.Sc. in Bioinformatics and a commercial tissue culture center supporting local banana farmers—and have contributed to the major inter-disciplinary BUILDER project. Supported by grants from national agencies like the DST, DBT, and UGC, our faculty publish high-impact research in international journals while helping students build successful careers in premier research institutions and multinational biotech companies.
