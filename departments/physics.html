Physics

Established in 1991 following the formation of Kuvempu University before shifting to the Shankaraghatta campus in 1994, our department offers postgraduate and doctoral programs in Physics with specializations in Nuclear Physics and Condensed Matter Physics. Backed by a Choice Based Credit System introduced in 2005-06, our qualified faculty guide active research in areas such as gas sensing, nonlinear optical properties of glasses, polymers and thin films, environmental radioactivity, and quantum information science. Supported by sophisticated laboratories and funding from various agencies, our researchers have produced 45 Ph.D. degrees and published numerous papers in international journals, while our students consistently clear competitive exams like UGC/CSIR-NET and GATE to pursue successful careers in research and academia.
