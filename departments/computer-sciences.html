Computer Sciences

Established in 2000 with the MCA program—recognized by AICTE—and followed in 2002 by the UGC-recognized M.Sc. in Computer Science, our department offers comprehensive postgraduate and doctoral programs combining pure and applied sciences. Supported by modern computer laboratories with 60 desktops and teaching aids, our four permanent assistant professors train students to meet the evolving demands of software companies, educational institutions, and research. Actively engaged in research areas such as digital image processing, pattern recognition, and data mining, we have awarded over 15 Ph.D. degrees and published around 86 research articles in peer-reviewed journals. Through workshops on emerging software tools, guest lectures by MNC professionals, national conferences like NCACC 12, and active alumni mentorship, we equip our students with essential technical skills and strong placement opportunities across software industries, academia, and IT entrepreneurship.
