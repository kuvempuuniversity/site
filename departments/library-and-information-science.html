Library and Information Science

Established in December 1993, our department offers a master’s degree in library and information science with a strong emphasis on IT-oriented areas and practical skills to meet modern societal needs. Supported by an independent computer laboratory with internet-enabled systems, we have produced over 300 graduates who hold prominent positions in central and state government departments, public and private enterprises, universities, R&D organizations, and multinational companies, with several moving abroad. Alongside active teaching, training, and consultancy initiatives—including hosting national seminars, conferences, and UGC-sponsored refresher courses—we also offer regular Ph.D. programs and distance education courses in library and information science.
