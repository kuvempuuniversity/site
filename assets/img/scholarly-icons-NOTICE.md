# Scholarly link icons

The controls are ordinary navigation links styled as buttons, not sign-in controls or provider-endorsed button widgets.

- Google Scholar: Google's hosted favicon, https://scholar.google.com/favicon.ico. Brand guidance: https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/.
- ORCID: ORCID's iD artwork distributed through https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ORCID_iD.svg, attributed there to ORCID and its Figshare asset. These manually supplied identifiers are labelled unauthenticated; no OAuth verification is claimed. ORCID, the ORCID logo and iD logo are trademarks of ORCID, Inc. Guidance: https://info.orcid.org/documentation/integration-guide/orcid-id-display-guidelines/.
- DOI: DOI Foundation identifier logo as reproduced at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:DOI_logo.svg (a traced reproduction, not a newly downloaded Foundation master). DOI is a trademark of the DOI Foundation. Full https://doi.org/ URLs remain visible beside the controls.

Icons are remotely hosted, displayed without recolouring, and use no-referrer requests. If an icon fails to load, its visible text link remains functional. The Commons copies are not claimed to be a provider-certified button kit. A future local-asset replacement should use current brand-library master files, including ORCID's distinct unauthenticated artwork when available; no icon should be presented as evidence of identity authentication.
