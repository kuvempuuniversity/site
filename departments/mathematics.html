Mathematics

Established in 1975 under the University of Mysore before becoming an integral part of Kuvempu University in 1987, our department offers rigorous postgraduate and doctoral programs in Mathematics, specializing in differential geometry, fluid mechanics, and computer-backed fluid flow simulation. Holding the distinction of producing the very first Ph.D. of Kuvempu University, we have guided over 30 doctoral scholars and published more than 350 research papers in reputed journals. Through active collaborations and training with premier institutions like IISc, TIFR, and IITs, alongside our student-focused initiatives like the Mathematical Society and unique 'earn and learn' schemes, we equip our students with the essential analytical and software skills required for successful careers in industry and academia.
