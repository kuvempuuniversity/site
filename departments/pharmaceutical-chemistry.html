Pharmaceutical Chemistry

Established in 2007, our department offers postgraduate and doctoral programs designed to supply skilled manpower for India's growing pharmaceutical industry. Guided by experienced faculty with international research exposure and post-doctoral experience across countries like England, Scotland, and Italy, our team has produced 2 Ph.D. degrees, secured 2 patents, and published over 40 research papers in national and international journals. Backed by research grants from agencies like the UGC, we actively involve M.Sc. students in research projects to help them secure lucrative scientific careers.
