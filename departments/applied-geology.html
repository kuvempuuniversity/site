Applied Geology

Established in 1993, our department offers comprehensive postgraduate and research programs in Earth Sciences, supported by around ₹120 lakhs in external funding from agencies like the University Grants Commission and the Department of Science and Technology under UGC-SAP, innovative programs, and DST-FIST grants. Guided by experienced regular and visiting faculty, our students utilize state-of-the-art laboratories equipped with sophisticated instruments like Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometers, advanced geospatial software suites such as ERDAS Imagine and ArcGIS, and modern field equipment like Total Stations and GPS for advanced water resource management and earth science studies.
