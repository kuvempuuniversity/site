Physical Education

Established in 2002-03 with the Master of Physical Education program followed in 2003-04 by the Bachelor of Physical Education (B.P.Ed.) degree for high school educators, our department offers comprehensive professional training and doctoral research programs in physical education and sports. Supported by qualified permanent faculty holding doctoral degrees, guest lecturers, and supportive staff, we provide robust academic instruction alongside professional development. Our campus infrastructure features an international-standard indoor sports complex, an upcoming 400-meter standard track, and dedicated outdoor playing facilities for football, hockey, tennis, kabaddi, kho-kho, and handball to support both curriculum practicals and student athletics. Recognized by NCTE and actively coordinating postgraduate diploma courses in sports management through distance education, our faculty guide doctoral research scholars and maintain active alumni networks to foster excellence in sports science and professional training.
