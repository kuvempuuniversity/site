Journalism and Mass Communication

Offering regular postgraduate and doctoral programs in Journalism and Mass Communication alongside a distance education PG Diploma in Kannada Journalism, our department combines rigorous theoretical study with hands-on professional training. We ensure academic excellence through regular workshops, special lectures by media experts, and mandatory internships with prominent media organizations. To provide real-world broadcast and print experience, students manage the publication of our daily laboratory newspaper, Sahyadri Times, and produce and broadcast news packages through our modern television studio on the Sahyadri TV YouTube channel.
