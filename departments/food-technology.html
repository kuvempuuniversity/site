Food Technology

Established in 2022 at the Jnana Sahyadri campus, our department offers M.Sc. and Ph.D. programs in Food Technology aimed at promoting innovation in the food sector. Guided by qualified faculty with international research exposure, our students build rewarding careers across food processing companies, soft drink manufacturing, quality control organizations, dairy industries, and leading multinational brands like Amul, Coca-Cola, Nestlé, Britannia, and ITC Limited, as well as premier research laboratories.
