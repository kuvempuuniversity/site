Political Science

Established in 1988, our department offers comprehensive postgraduate and doctoral programs in Political Science, operating under a Choice Based Credit System since 2005-2006 with a curriculum spanning 20 papers and compulsory project work. We maintain active national collaborations with institutions like CSDS in Delhi and CSCS in Bengaluru for election surveys and seminars, alongside international partnerships with Ghent University in Belgium that led to participation in the European Union's prestigious Asia-Link Programme. Housing two specialized government-sanctioned study chairs—the Sri Abdul Nazeer Sab Chair for Panchayat Raj and Decentralization and the Sri Shantaveri Gopal Gowda Chair for Parliamentary Democracy—we actively engage in extension activities, hosting workshops and training programs for elected representatives and local panchayat officials, alongside offering distance education programs in political science and Panchayati Raj.
