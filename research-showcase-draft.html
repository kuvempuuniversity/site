How to read this working draft

The scope is the University as a whole. The examples are replaceable placeholders drawn from records already retrieved, not confirmed selections or a complete institutional assessment. No fresh citation retrieval, paid provider calls or automatic selection jobs are enabled by this page.

Rules to agree before publishing verified selections
  1. Reconcile researcher identities and department assignments across the university roster. Deduplicate people appearing in more than one role.
  2. Use a consistent paper-level citation source for a comparison. The current website uses Google Scholar for Environmental Science and available OpenAlex records elsewhere; those sources are not combined into a uniform ranking here.
  3. Agree the reporting period, eligible output types, staff categories, minimum paper counts, minimum data coverage and treatment of ties.
  4. Deduplicate shared papers within departmental and university totals, while crediting their relevant co-authors.
  5. Display sources, retrieval dates, eligible paper counts, matching coverage and the denominator used. Missing records must not silently become zeroes.
  6. Review selections before describing them as university-wide leaders. Raw citations and per-paper or per-staff averages are not field-normalised measures or complete assessments of research quality.
Attribution and interpretation

Researcher records may include work completed before joining Kuvempu University or while associated with another institution. These examples describe the scholarly records of the listed researchers; they do not claim that every output was produced at the University.

The eventual showcase can recognise several kinds of scholarly contribution rather than declaring an overall best researcher or department. Books, datasets, research stories and policy or community contributions can be considered as supporting evidence becomes available.

Existing department pages, citation datasets and retrieval schedules are unchanged by this draft-page update.
