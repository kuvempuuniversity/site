Urdu

Established in December 1993, our department is dedicated to advanced postgraduate teaching and doctoral research in Urdu, Persian, and Arabic. To honour academic excellence, we have instituted four gold medals recognizing top performers in M.A. Previous, Persian, Arabic, and overall M.A. final scores, while our students consistently clear competitive examinations like NET, SLET, IAS, KAS, and KES through dedicated faculty coaching. Supported by an extensive departmental library housing over 1,200 books and 200 reputed journals and magazines, we foster literary and cultural engagement by regularly organizing annual multilingual mushairas alongside our rigorous academic programs.
