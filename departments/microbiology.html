Microbiology

Established in 2005, our department offers comprehensive postgraduate and research programs in Microbiology, featuring well-equipped laboratories, seminar halls, and a curriculum focused on practical methodologies. Our faculty specialize in industrial and environmental microbiology, plant pathology, agricultural microbiology, immunology, and medical microbiology, backed by international research exposure and funding from agencies like the DST, DBT, UGC, and VGST. Through special lecture series, national seminars featuring experts from premier institutes like IISc, CCMB, and NCL, and specialized research on regional concerns such as Kyasanur Forest Disease, we empower students to successfully clear national exams like CSIR and SLET while building impactful careers in research and industry.
