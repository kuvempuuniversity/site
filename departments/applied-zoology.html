Applied Zoology

Established in 1993, our department offers M.Sc. in Zoology and Ph.D. in Applied Zoology, focusing on core areas like genetics, biodiversity, and toxicology. We train students across molecular biology, biotechnology, and computational biology, supported by a strong placement record. Our faculty have successfully executed research projects funded by national and state agencies, including the DST, DBT, UGC, and the Vision Group on Science and Technology, alongside contributing to the inter-disciplinary BUILDER project.
