About this website and research metrics

A student-first website concept. Research figures describe available records, not a university ranking or a complete institutional research assessment.

Whose research is represented?

Department collections combine the teaching team and published PhD-student profiles listed on this website. Records can predate a researcher’s appointment or originate at other institutions. These figures must not be interpreted as research conducted exclusively at Kuvempu University.

Publication and PhD figures

The research paper counter includes source-classified journal articles, reviews and full conference papers. Other outputs remain listed but are excluded from the counter. Matching shared papers are counted once within a department. Metadata and coverage can be incomplete.

PhDs awarded are supplied separately from publication records, not inferred from researchers’ qualifications or current student profiles. Environmental Science currently displays 39, a provisional departmental total supplied by the user on 31 October 2026, pending verification from departmental records. Provisional figures are not presented here as independently verified totals. An unavailable total is shown as a dash, not zero.

Citation retrieval and coverage

The strip uses the label “Citations to these papers”. For Environmental Science, the source is Google Scholar, retrieved through the third-party provider SerpApi, not an official Google API. Other departments continue to use available validated, cached OpenAlex counts. Google Scholar and OpenAlex values are not added together or silently substituted.

Scholar articles are matched to the departmental research-paper collection using exact normalised titles and publication years with supporting author-name overlap. This is automated matching, not independent verification of every record. Each matched paper contributes once; individual author-profile totals are not added together. The sum counts citations to papers, not distinct citing documents across the department.

The initial Environmental Science snapshot was retrieved on 31 October 2026 at 20:11 IST (14:41 UTC): 1,788 citations across 45 matched papers out of 72 eligible papers. Five public profiles were retrieved using seven provider requests. Thirteen listed researchers lacked a usable recorded Scholar profile. Unmatched records, unavailable values and conflicting observations are excluded, not treated as confirmed zero-citation papers. Successful retrieval of available profiles does not establish complete departmental coverage.

Paper-level records retain source links and retrieval dates. Only snapshot records still matching the eligible paper collection contribute to its total. This paper-level Scholar snapshot is not automatically refreshed. Source coverage, matching and dates differ; totals from different sources are not directly comparable, and a higher total alone does not establish greater research impact.

Supplementary explanation: publication word clouds

Clouds show recurring words and short phrases from available research-paper titles. Larger, bolder terms occur in more distinct paper titles; repetitions within one title do not increase its contribution. Common filler words and selected location names are excluded. These are indicative title terms, not author-supplied keywords or measures of research impact.

The most frequent selected term is centred, with the others arranged decoratively around it. Positions do not measure topic relationships. Type and spacing adapt to the compact panel; less frequent terms may be omitted where necessary to avoid overlap. The paper records and research counts are not changed by this visual selection. On exceptionally narrow layouts, a readable scrolling list is used instead.
